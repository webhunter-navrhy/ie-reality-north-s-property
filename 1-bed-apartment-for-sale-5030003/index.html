NORTH'S PROPERTY presents Apt 14, 6 Ushers Quay, a well-presented first-floor one-bedroom apartment situated within this mature and well-established residential development.

Extending to approximately 40 sq. m. (430 sq. ft.), the apartment offers well-proportioned accommodation comprising an entrance hallway, spacious open-plan living/ kitchen area, generous double bedroom, bathroom, hotpress and additional storage.

The bright and welcoming living/kitchen area is positioned to the rear of the apartment and benefits from large windows, excellent natural light and private balcony away from the hustle and bustle of cthe city. The light-coloured décor and wood-effect laminate flooring further enhance the sense of space and brightness.

The spacious double bedroom has fitted wardrobes provide excellent storage while maximising the available floor space.

The bathroom is fully tiled and features contrasting wall and floor tiling, with an electric shower.

A hotpress and separate storage area are conveniently located off the entrance hallway. Heating throughout the apartment is electric, with updated heaters fitted, while the windows are PVC double glazed.

The development benefits from secure entrance doors and gates, with intercom systems serving the entrance and common areas.
